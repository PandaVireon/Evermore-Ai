EVERMORE POLICY

Refund Policy

Evermore is committed to maintaining a clear, secure, and transparent payment process. This policy sets out the requirements and review process applicable to refund requests for Evermore memberships and related payments.

Last updated · 2026

Refund verification requirement

Before an approved refund can be processed, the requester will be required to provide their NIN details linked to the bank account used for the original payment. This verification is intended to help confirm the identity of the account holder and protect against fraudulent refund requests.

1. Refund requests

Refund requests should be submitted through the official Evermore support channel and should include enough information for the payment to be identified, such as your name, email address, phone number, package selected, amount paid, and available payment reference or proof of payment.

2. Identity and bank-account verification

Before any approved refund is processed, Evermore may require the requester to provide their NIN details linked to the bank account used for the original payment. The submitted information must correspond with the account used for the payment being reviewed. Refund processing may be delayed where the required verification information is incomplete, inconsistent, or cannot be verified.

Please provide verification information only through the official Evermore support channel. Do not send NIN details to unofficial contacts or third parties claiming to represent Evermore.

3. Review of payments

Each request may be reviewed against the payment details and the status of the related Evermore service. A refund request does not automatically mean that a refund will be issued. Evermore may request additional information where necessary to verify a payment or resolve a dispute.

4. Duplicate or mistaken payments

If you believe you paid more than once for the same membership or sent a payment in error, contact support as soon as possible with the relevant payment information so the transaction can be reviewed.

5. Fraudulent or unauthorized payments

If you believe a payment was made without your authorization, contact your payment provider promptly and also notify Evermore support. Evermore may request documentation needed to investigate the transaction.

6. Processing

Where a refund is approved, the timing of the refund may depend on the payment method, banking institution, and payment-processing channel used for the original transaction.

7. Changes to this policy

Evermore may update this policy when necessary. The current version published on this page will be the version applicable to future requests.

8. Important payment guidance

Only make payments through the payment instructions presented by the official Evermore website. If you are unsure about a payment instruction, stop and contact Evermore through an official support channel before sending funds.

Need help with a refund?

Contact Evermore support with your payment details and refund request. Our support team can review the transaction and advise you on the next steps.
