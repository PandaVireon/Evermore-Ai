EVERMORE PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Evermore respects the information entrusted to the platform and is committed to handling personal information with care, transparency, and appropriate security practices.

Last updated · 2026

Your information matters

Evermore aims to collect only information that is relevant to providing, supporting, securing, and improving the services and payment processes presented on the website.

1. Information you provide

Depending on how you use Evermore, you may provide information such as your full name, email address, phone number, selected membership package, payment information, payment reference, support messages, and information voluntarily provided when requesting a refund or resolving a payment issue.

2. Membership details

When you use the membership and payment flow, the details you enter may be used to carry your selected package and contact information between the relevant pages and to prepare support or payment-confirmation messages.

The current membership flow stores customer-entered details in the browser for continuity of the checkout experience. Customer details are not used to create a general public profile.

3. Payment and refund information

Payment-related information may be used to identify a transaction, confirm payment, provide activation support, investigate disputes, and process eligible refund requests.

For refund verification, Evermore may require NIN details linked to the bank account used for the original payment. Such information should be provided only through an official Evermore support channel when specifically requested for verification.

4. Receipt uploads

When the payment page allows a payment receipt to be selected, the receipt is handled by the browser as part of the confirmation flow. The current website flow does not intentionally create a permanent customer receipt archive in the public-facing website.

Users should avoid uploading unrelated documents or information that is not necessary for the payment-confirmation process.

5. How information is used

  • To provide and operate Evermore services and membership flows.
  • To communicate with you about payments, activation, support, and requests you submit.
  • To verify transactions and investigate suspected misuse or unauthorized activity.
  • To process and review refund requests.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the platform.
  • To administer platform configuration and service operations.

6. Information sharing

Evermore does not present customer information as publicly visible profile information. Information may be disclosed when necessary to operate a requested service, process a payment or refund, respond to a lawful request, investigate suspected fraud or misuse, or protect the rights, safety, and security of the platform and its users.

7. Third-party services

Evermore may rely on third-party infrastructure and communication or payment-related services to operate parts of the website. Those services may process information according to their own terms and privacy practices. Users should review the relevant third-party policies where applicable.

8. Security

Evermore uses technical and operational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should also protect their devices, accounts, and payment information.

9. Your choices

You may choose not to provide information requested by the platform, but certain services, payment confirmations, support actions, or refund reviews may not be possible without the information required for that purpose.

10. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including transaction support, dispute handling, security, operational records, and legal or regulatory requirements where applicable.

11. Policy updates

Evermore may update this Privacy Policy as the platform, its services, or its information practices develop. The latest version published on this page will reflect the current policy.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact Evermore through the official support email. Please do not send sensitive identity information unless it is specifically required for an identified support or verification request.

Have a question?

For questions about Evermore, memberships, payments, privacy, or support, contact the Evermore team through the official support email.
